The Heart Muscle Metaphor
A = Heart = Parasympathetic = Home
your core self, stillness, where you actually live
B = Muscle = Sympathetic = Beta
your surface self, the functional layer that interacts with the world, protects A, keeps you employed and off grocery floors 😭
Together = Myocardium = just you, no difference

The Spectrum:
⬅️ too much A = Bradycardia = too calm, doing nothing, floor sitting risk
✅ balanced = Normal Sinus Rhythm = healthy hybrid, your target baseline
➡️ too much B = Tachycardia = anxious, exhausted, A getting starved

Diagnostics:

check anxiety level, too calm = brady, too anxious = tachy

Interventions:

brady → Epinephrine = activate B, go do something
tachy → Beta Blocker = meditate, let A fill back up
